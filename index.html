02 — ABOUT

Architecture, cost, and schedule — grounded in construction.

I’m Arnold Consigo, a Licensed Architect based in the Philippines with hands-on construction experience across design, documentation, site coordination, estimating, scheduling, progress monitoring, and project delivery.

My primary professional focus is the intersection of three disciplines: architecture, construction estimating, and project scheduling. My construction background strengthens all three by keeping design decisions, quantities, costs, sequencing, and timelines connected to real site conditions.

This combination allows me to support projects beyond a single trade — from understanding the drawings, to quantifying the work, to building a practical schedule and monitoring how the project moves toward delivery.
